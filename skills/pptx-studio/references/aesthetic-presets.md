# PPTX aesthetic presets

Use these presets to keep decks visually coherent and presentation-ready.

## modern-product

- Use for product launches, roadmap updates, and operating reviews.
- Strong accent color, high contrast titles, generous white space.
- Good default for mixed executive + IC audiences.

## investor-clean

- Use for fundraising, board decks, and financial storytelling.
- Conservative palette and compact content density.
- Emphasize clear section labels and concise bullets.

## academic-minimal

- Use for research, methodology explainers, and technical talks.
- Neutral palette and restrained ornamentation.
- Prioritize legibility over decorative style.

## Slide craft rules

- Keep one core idea per slide.
- Limit bullets to 3-5 lines per slide when possible.
- Keep title language action-oriented and specific.
- Use callout boxes for key metrics, not full paragraphs.
