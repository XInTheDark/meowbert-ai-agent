# DOCX aesthetic presets

Use these presets for consistently styled output from `docx_create_styled_document`.

## modern-report

- Best for product updates, strategy docs, and decision memos.
- Headings: dark slate blue, semibold, clear vertical rhythm.
- Body: neutral dark gray with generous paragraph spacing.
- Bullet style: lightweight and scannable.

## executive-brief

- Best for stakeholder summaries and board-facing briefs.
- Headings: deep navy, stronger contrast, tighter hierarchy.
- Body: compact spacing to keep high information density.
- Bullet style: concise one-line statements.

## academic-clean

- Best for research summaries and technical write-ups.
- Headings: monochrome, minimal decoration.
- Body: justified text and traditional typography cadence.
- Bullet style: balanced spacing suitable for long reading.

## Style guidance

- Keep heading depth shallow (`H1` + optional `H2` semantics in text) for readability.
- Keep paragraphs under ~110 words when possible.
- Keep bullet points parallel in grammar and tense.
- Use one preset per document; avoid mixing visual systems.
